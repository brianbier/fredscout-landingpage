Project Summary — FretScout
Used & vintage guitar discovery, deal valuation, and alerts — with a conversational option.
1) What FretScout is
FretScout is a discovery + intelligence platform for used/vintage guitars.
It helps buyers find the right guitar faster by searching across multiple sources, normalizing messy listings, estimating fair market value, and alerting users the moment a great match appears.
Important clarification: FretScout does not handle transactions or payments. When a user wants to buy, they click out to the partner site (e.g., marketplace or shop) to complete the purchase.
2) The problem / pain point
Buying used/vintage is high-friction for a few reasons:
Fragmented inventory: listings are spread across marketplaces and individual shop sites.
Inconsistent information: model names, year/spec details, and condition terms vary wildly.
Hard to judge value: prices vary based on year, condition, mods, scarcity, shipping, and seller trust.
Time-sensitive market: the best deals disappear quickly; checking multiple sites is a chore.
3) The solution
FretScout solves this by combining four things in one place:
A) Unified search across used/vintage sources
A single place to search inventory across initial, permission-friendly sources and opt-in shops
Clean filters that matter for used gear (condition, year range, shipping vs pickup, radius, etc.)
“All-in” pricing (including shipping) so comparisons are real
B) Normalization & matching (turn messy listings into structured data)
Translate inconsistent listing text into standardized attributes:
brand/model/variant, approximate year, key specs, normalized condition, location
Deduplicate duplicates and reduce noisy/irrelevant results
C) Deal valuation (“Deal Score”) with confidence
Every listing gets a simple, explainable assessment:
Deal Score: Good / Fair / High relative to a fair market range
Confidence indicator: High/Medium/Low based on how clearly the listing matches a known model + condition
Transparent “why” (e.g., “priced ~10% below typical range for this model/condition; shipping included”)
D) Alerts (the habit-forming wedge)
Save a search and get notified when:
a matching listing appears
a listing drops into your target range
a “Good deal” shows up (optional)
4) What we offer (core product experience)
FretScout can be used in two complementary ways:
Primary: Fast discovery + scoring + alerts
Search or browse, get ranked results with Deal Scores, set alerts
Optional layer: Conversational guidance (experience, not the product)
Instead of forcing users to learn filters, an assistant can help translate intent into structured search and recommendations:
“I want a 2003 Taylor 114.”
“I want a cheap American-made solid-wood acoustic under $600 from a leading brand.”
The assistant asks a small number of clarifying questions, runs the same underlying search/valuation/alerts engine, and explains recommendations.
Guardrail: Any “gem” or historical/spec claim should be grounded in a curated knowledge base and/or reputable sources; otherwise the assistant frames it as a hypothesis and lowers confidence.
5) Who it’s for (initial target users)
Enthusiasts & collectors hunting specific models/colors/years
Deal hunters who want underpriced listings fast

Working musicians who want reliable used options with less time spent searching
(Later) shops/sellers who benefit from market intelligence and lead flow
6) Differentiation (why we win)
Focus on used/vintage, where fragmentation and price dispersion are real
Deal valuation + confidence built into the shopping flow (not just “search results”)
Alerts create repeat engagement and real buyer advantage
Over time, a curated guitar knowledge layer enables “smart recommendations” (not just lowest price)
7) Initial scope and constraints (to keep it real)
In scope (early):
Discovery/search + normalization + deal score + alerts
Click-out to partner sites to complete purchases (no checkout)
Out of scope (early):
Payment processing, escrow, shipping, dispute resolution
Hosting user-generated listings
Perfect valuation for heavily modded/partscaster listings (we’ll score with lower confidence)
8) One-sentence positioning
“Find used/vintage guitars across sources, see which listings are truly good deals, and get alerted the moment the right one shows up.”
